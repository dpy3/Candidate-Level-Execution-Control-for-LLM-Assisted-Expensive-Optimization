# Narrative hardening pass

Frozen: 2026-07-20.

This pass does not add experiments or call an external model API. It reduces
submission misinterpretation risk while preserving the existing evidence:

- The title presents Controlled Semantic Admission first; L-ADE-CSA remains a DE
  reference implementation.
- The abstract and keywords foreground host authority, record-level traceability,
  and fail-closed evaluation rather than application domains or ranking claims.
- Provenance is explicitly limited to non-cryptographic, record-level lineage
  inside the trusted host process.
- Model names in the protocol table are identified as gateway-reported IDs and
  are not presented as independently verified vendor deployment identities.
- Five historical RQs are compressed into three evidence questions: authority
  closure, portability/boundary, and bounded utility.
- CEC, WSN, and civilian route-planning results are labelled descriptive context;
  final acquisition superiority remains unclaimed.

The final source package was independently compiled from its own source
directory after synchronization. The resulting main manuscript is 21 pages with
zero LaTeX errors, undefined references/citations, or overfull boxes.
